Visible-light transport

The camera is the cable.

The sender never opens a socket and never uploads the file. It renders the file as binary QR frames. The receiver watches those frames through the camera, accepts chunks in any order, rejects corrupted frames, and reconstructs the original file locally.

01

Select & encode

Choose a local file. QRFerry prepares the payload, optionally compresses it, and splits it into numbered QR chunks.

02

Scan the stream

The sender repeats animated QR frames while the receiving device collects unique chunks through its camera.

03

Verify & rebuild

QRFerry checks CRC32 integrity, reconstructs the file locally, then lets you preview, save, or share it.

Private by transport

No application server in the middle.

Your file stays on the sending and receiving devices during QRFerry's transfer flow. The optical stream is the transport itself. The current protocol is intentionally simple and uses CRC32 for error detection, not encryption or cryptographic authentication.
