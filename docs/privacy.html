01

What QRFerry collects

No account profile, analytics stream, or advertising identifier.

QRFerry itself does not collect, sell, rent, or transmit personal information to an application backend. The current app has no sign-in system, advertising SDK, analytics SDK, remote database, or QRFerry-operated server.

Personal profile

Not collected

No name, email address, phone number, account, or profile is required by QRFerry.

Analytics / ads

Not collected

QRFerry does not include advertising or analytics SDKs in the current release.

Location

Not requested

The app does not request device location permission for file transfer.

Network transfer

Not required

File transfer happens through visible QR frames rather than an application network connection.

02

Camera access

Used to receive QR frames and rebuild the selected file locally.

QRFerry requests camera permission on a receiving device so it can scan the animated QR stream shown on the sending device. Camera frames are processed for QR decoding on the device.

QRFerry does not require the camera for sending a file, and the app does not intentionally upload camera images or video to a QRFerry server. You can deny or revoke camera access in your device settings, but receiving files by QR code will not work without it.

03

Files and transfer data

Your selected file stays within the sending and receiving devices.

When sending, you choose a file through the system file picker. QRFerry reads that file locally, optionally compresses it, splits it into chunks, and renders those bytes as repeating QR frames.

Transfer frames can contain technical metadata needed to reconstruct the file, including the filename, file size, chunk information, a transfer-session identifier, compression state, and CRC32 integrity values. The receiving device processes these frames locally and reconstructs the file on that device.

After reception, QRFerry may store the reconstructed file in the app's local documents area so you can preview, save, or share it. Files remain under your device's control until you delete or export them.

04

Optical privacy is not encryption

Keep the QR stream visible only to devices you intend to receive the file.

QRFerry avoids cloud transport, but its current transfer protocol is not end-to-end encrypted. QR frames are visible on the sender's screen. A nearby person or camera with a clear view of the complete stream may be able to capture enough frames to reconstruct the transferred content.

CRC32 values are used to detect transfer errors; they are not cryptographic authentication or tamper protection. Do not treat the current protocol as secure encryption for highly sensitive material.

QR

For private transfers, control who can see the sending screen, keep devices close together, and avoid displaying the QR stream in public or monitored areas.

05

Sharing and third parties

Nothing is shared unless you choose an external destination.

QRFerry can open your operating system's native share/save interface for a received file. If you choose another app, cloud provider, messaging service, storage location, or person from that interface, the selected destination may receive the file under its own privacy practices.

QRFerry does not control how a third-party app or service handles data after you explicitly share or save content to it.

06

Retention and deletion

QRFerry has no remote account or cloud record to delete.

Because QRFerry does not maintain a remote account or application backend, there is no QRFerry cloud profile or server-side transfer history. Files reconstructed on your device remain local until you remove them, uninstall the app, or manage them through the available system storage controls.

Data you export to another location is governed by that location or service and may remain there after QRFerry is removed.

07

Children's privacy

No child-specific account or behavioral profiling.

QRFerry does not knowingly collect personal information from children through an account, analytics system, advertising system, or QRFerry-operated backend. The app's local file-transfer functionality works the same way regardless of age.

08

Changes to this policy

The policy will evolve if QRFerry's data behavior changes.

This Privacy Policy may be updated when QRFerry adds or changes features, permissions, storage behavior, or third-party services. Material changes will be reflected in an updated effective date on this page.

If a future release adds online services, analytics, accounts, advertising, or other data collection, this policy should be updated before that release is distributed.
