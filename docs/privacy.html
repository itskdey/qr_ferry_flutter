01

What QRFerry collects

No account profile, analytics stream, advertising identifier, or QRFerry backend.

QRFerry itself does not collect, sell, rent, or transmit personal information to an application backend. The current app does not include a sign-in system, advertising SDK, analytics SDK, remote database, or QRFerry-operated server.

Personal profile

Not collected

No name, email address, phone number, account, or user profile is required.

Analytics / ads

Not collected

The current release does not include advertising or analytics SDKs.

Location

Not requested

QRFerry does not request device location permission for transfers.

Network transport

Not required

File bytes move through visible QR frames rather than a QRFerry network connection.

02

Camera access

Used only to scan incoming QR frames on the receiving device.

QRFerry requests camera permission when you receive a file. The camera is used to scan the animated QR stream shown on the sending device. Camera frames are processed on-device for QR decoding.

QRFerry does not require camera access for sending and does not intentionally upload camera images or video to a QRFerry server. If you revoke camera permission, receiving files by QR code will not work.

03

Files and transfer data

The selected file remains between the sending and receiving devices.

When sending, QRFerry reads the file you choose through the system file picker, may compress it locally, splits it into chunks, and renders those bytes as repeating QR frames.

Frames can contain technical metadata required to rebuild the transfer, including the filename, file size, chunk information, transfer session identifier, compression state, and CRC32 integrity values.

After reception, the reconstructed file can be stored locally so you can preview, save, or share it. The file stays under your device's control until you delete or export it.

04

Optical privacy is not encryption

Keep the QR stream visible only to the device you intend to receive it.

QRFerry avoids cloud transport, but the current transfer protocol is not end-to-end encrypted. QR frames are visible on the sender's display. A nearby person or camera with a clear view of the complete stream may be able to capture enough frames to reconstruct the transferred content.

CRC32 is used for transfer error detection. It is not cryptographic authentication, encryption, or tamper protection.

QR

For private transfers, keep the sending screen out of view of other people and cameras, keep the devices close together, and avoid broadcasting sensitive QR streams in public areas.

05

Sharing and third parties

External apps receive a file only when you explicitly choose to share or save it.

QRFerry can open the operating system's native share/save interface. If you choose another app, cloud provider, messaging service, storage location, or person, that destination may receive the file under its own privacy practices.

QRFerry does not control how third-party apps or services handle content after you explicitly export it.

06

Retention and deletion

No remote QRFerry account or server-side transfer history exists in the current release.

QRFerry does not maintain a remote user profile or cloud transfer record. Files reconstructed on your device remain local until you remove them, uninstall the app, or manage them through your device's storage controls.

Content you export elsewhere may remain at that destination according to the destination's own retention rules.

07

Children's privacy

No child-specific account, advertising profile, or behavioral tracking.

QRFerry does not knowingly collect personal information from children through an account, analytics system, advertising system, or QRFerry-operated backend. Its local file-transfer functionality works the same way regardless of age.

08

Policy changes

This page will change if QRFerry's data behavior changes.

This Privacy Policy may be updated when QRFerry adds or changes features, permissions, storage behavior, or third-party services. Material changes will be reflected by an updated effective date on this page.

If a future release introduces online services, accounts, analytics, advertising, or other data collection, this policy should be updated before that release is distributed.
